A car moving at 72 km/h brakes on a dry road with friction coefficient μ = 0.5. Find the stopping distance and the braking time. JPGproblem-photo.jpg
Step-by-step solution
  1. 1
    Convert speed: 72 km/h ÷ 3.6v₀ = 20 m/s
  2. 2
    Friction is the only horizontal force, so the deceleration isa = μg = 0.5 · 9.81 = 4.905 m/s²
  3. 3
    Final speed is 0, so use the time-free equation Lecture 4 · (4.3)d = v₀² / 2a = 400 / 9.81 ≈ 40.8 m
  4. 4
    Time from Lecture 4 · (4.1)t = v₀ / a ≈ 4.08 s
Answer: d ≈ 40.8 m, t ≈ 4.08 s
