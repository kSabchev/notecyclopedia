Uniformly accelerated motion

Lecture · Prof. [LECTURER]Edited yesterday by youRevision 6
Summarize Edit

A body moves with uniform acceleration when its velocity changes by equal amounts in equal intervals of time. The acceleration vector is constant in both magnitude and direction, which makes the motion fully predictable from three numbers: the initial position, the initial velocity, and the acceleration.

1 · Equations of motion

v = v₀ + at velocity at time t (4.1) Calculate
s = v₀t + ½at² displacement (4.2) Calculate
v² = v₀² + 2as time-free form (4.3) Calculate

Eliminating t between (4.1) and (4.2) gives (4.3), which is the quickest route whenever a problem gives distances and speeds but no time.

My note Watch the sign of a when braking — choose the positive direction first, then everything opposite to it is negative. Lost points on this in the mock exam.

2 · Worked example

A car starting from rest reaches 25 m/s in 10 s. Its acceleration is a = (25 − 0)/10 = 2.5 m/s², and by (4.2) it covers s = ½ · 2.5 · 10² = 125 m.
